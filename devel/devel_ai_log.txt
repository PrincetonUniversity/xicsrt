  warm. Plain numpy wins at this scale on CPU regardless of retracing fix.
- Reverted the import and deleted tools_jax/test_voigt_jax.py rather than
  leaving it unwired, since no viable speedup path was found.

Key findings / gotchas:
- pytest passing does not catch this class of bug: tests only ever called
  the sampler with fixed/repeated shapes, always hitting the jit cache.
- JAX per-call dispatch/compile overhead is not recovered by small
  per-bundle workloads (few lines, single/double-digit ray counts) on CPU;
  crossover point for GPU or much larger line lists not evaluated.
