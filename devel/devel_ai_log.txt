2026-07-28 — F006 planning: diagnosed the large-run memory/tail problem.

Files modified:
- devel/plan_raytrace_memory.md (new, approved plan)
- devel/features_request.md (F006 added, Pending)

Key changes:
- Analysis/planning session only; no source files touched.

Key findings / gotchas:
- User's hypothesis (combine_raytrace is the bottleneck) is wrong: ~195 MB
  combined history at 1e6 detected rays, and with keep_history=False both
  _sort_raytrace and combine_raytrace history blocks are skipped entirely by
  their guards (xicsrt_raytrace.py:254 and :359). Neither ran in that run.
- The reported low-CPU/rising-memory tail is swap: 12 workers x ~6 GB vs
  32 GB physical. Not an XICSRT bug; will not occur on Stellar at 768 GB.
- Memory is not the 1e9-ray constraint: 195 B/ray history (65 B/ray x 3
  elements) + ~110 B/ray temporaries + ~2x transient => ~26 GB at 12 workers.
  number_of_iter=19 reaches 1e9 rays with no code change and no memory
  increase; raising time_resolution instead would multiply the peak.
- np.einsum does NOT use threaded BLAS: zero speedup 1->8 threads on the
  entire hot path ('ij,ij->i', 'ij,ijk->ik'), while a 2000^3 gemm scaled
  62->33.5 ms. 12 runs x 8 threads uses ~12 of 96 cores. User kept 12 runs.
- np.random.shuffle perturbs the global RNG stream N-dependently, so a naive
  1:1 regression test would fail on iteration 2+ after any _sort_raytrace
  change. A dedicated np.random.Generator avoids this (verified), keeping
  found rays bit-identical. Hence the Tier A (exact) / Tier B (statistical)
  harness split, and number_of_iter=3 as the divergence detector.
- Rejected the 610x rejection sampler for _sort_raytrace in favor of the 21x
  Generator.choice+flatnonzero: at 0.013% of an iteration the extra speed is
  unmeasurable and not worth the retry loop / np.unique bug surface.
- Pre-existing defects found: combine_raytrace drops 'weight' (resolves the
  jaxrt_sync.md:164 quirk when fixed); worker profiler_results are never
  returned to the parent; 'mp: gathering' times .get() on already-finished
  results and measures nothing.

Suggested follow-ups:
- Implement F006 per devel/plan_raytrace_memory.md (harness first).
- Out of scope, worth its own entry: per-element history compaction would cut
  history 195 -> ~65 B/ray and raise the in-flight ceiling ~3x.
