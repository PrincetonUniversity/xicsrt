  dicts, float64 (x64 mode), same JSON configs, jax as optional extra.
- Poisson under jit: capacity + mask (exact Poisson N, capacity = mean + 10

Key findings / gotchas:

  hazards are global np.random, in-place masked writes, rejection sampler in

- Existing jax footholds: tools/xicsrt_math_jax.py, tools/xicsrt_faddeeva.py.
- Multiprocessing + JAX intentionally excluded (recompile per fork, GPU
  memory contention); cross-node scaling via SLURM arrays + seeds.
